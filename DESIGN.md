# Little Studio — DESIGN

## Layout (0.6)
Creative-tool workspace, not dashboard.
- Top command bar tabs: Yaz · Havuz · Kuyruk · İstatistik · Otomasyon · Bağlantılar
- Home = **composer desk** (story dominates), phone + status rail on the right
- Recent stories = dense list rows under composer (no card grid)
- Pool = 3 columns (Hazır / Yükleniyor / Yayında)
- Settings = left section nav + single panel

## Palette
Felt rail `#24382e`, paper `#f7f3ea`, coral CTA `#e25b4a`, Nunito self-host.

## Rejected from UI/UX Pro Max defaults
OLED dark / Inter+Playfair / purple cinema — conflict with PRODUCT warm atelier and brand rules.
Kept: primary-action hierarchy, empty states with CTA, Spectrum-like density for operator tools.
